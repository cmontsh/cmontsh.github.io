01 / About

Science grounded in the field.
Innovation aimed at impact.

I’m Mthobisi Dhladhla, an agriculture researcher and emerging agri-tech builder. My work sits where plant science, climate adaptation, data and practical agricultural innovation meet.

I’m particularly interested in underutilised indigenous plant resources, water-use efficiency, crop resilience, agricultural data systems and technologies that make science more useful to farmers.
